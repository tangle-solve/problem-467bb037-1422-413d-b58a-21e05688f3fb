### CLIM-000019: The climate signal-to-noise paradox
- **Subfield:** Climate variability and atmospheric circulation
- **Open:** Explain why some initialized ensembles predict observations better than their apparent predictable signal implies. Identify and correct weak responses, excessive noise, sampling, or ensemble-construction causes.
- **Status:** Prominent since 2014; formalized in 2018; a 2024 community reassessment found no conclusive general resolution.
- **Type:** theory
- **Why:** Could unlock additional seasonal and decadal forecast skill.
- **Key works:** Scaife and Smith (2018), [“A signal-to-noise paradox in climate science”](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41612-018-0038-4); Weisheimer et al. (2024), [“The Signal-to-Noise Paradox in Climate Forecasts: Revisiting Our Understanding and Identifying Future Priorities”](https://doi.org/10.1175/BAMS-D-24-0019.1).
- **Related:** Ensemble calibration under misspecification; weak-signal estimation.
